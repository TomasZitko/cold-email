## 🎯 Key Improvements Based on Research

### 1. **REDESIGN PROBABILITY SCORING (30 points) - NEW**

**Research Finding:** "Websites redesigned every 3-5 years"

**V3 Implementation:**
- **3-5 year old websites: 30 points** (PERFECT redesign timing)
- **5-7 year old: 25 points** (overdue, high probability)
- **0-2 year old: 0 points** (recently redesigned, skip)
- **Bonus points for:**
  - DIY template sites (Wix, Weebly, Squarespace): +12pts
  - Old copyright (≤2020): +8pts
  - Flash/deprecated tech: +10pts
  - Not mobile-friendly: +8pts

**Why This Matters:**
- Targets businesses at the RIGHT moment in redesign cycle
- DIY users are ready to upgrade to professional
- Old copyright = owner knows site is neglected

---

### 2. **INDUSTRY TIER SYSTEM (30 points max) - COMPLETELY REWEIGHTED**

**Research Finding:** "B2B professional services = $8k-$15k projects, 10-15% conversion"

#### New 9-Tier System:

| Tier | Industry Type | Points | Avg Project | Conversion | Examples |
|------|---------------|--------|-------------|------------|----------|
| **1** | Premium B2B Professional | 30 | $12,000 | 10-15% | Legal, Financial, Medical |
| **2** | Visual & Creative Premium | 28 | $10,000 | 8-12% | Real Estate, Architecture |
| **3** | Coaching & Online Services | 25 | $7,500 | 7-10% | Coaches, Online Courses |
| **4** | E-commerce & Online Retail | 22 | $6,000 | 6-9% | E-shops, Online Stores |
| **5** | Premium Wellness & Health | 20 | $4,500 | 5-8% | Spa, Wellness Centers |
| **6** | Hospitality & Tourism | 17 | $4,000 | 5-7% | Hotels, Restaurants |
| **7** | Professional Local Services | 14 | $3,000 | 4-6% | Hair Salons, Auto Repair |
| **8** | Established Local Services | 10 | $2,500 | 3-5% | Construction, Plumbing |
| **9** | Basic Local B2C | 5 | $1,500 | 2-4% | Cafes, Bars, Shops |

**Key Changes:**
- **Tier 1 (Legal, Financial, Medical):** 30pts vs old 15pts → **2X increase**
- **Tier 9 (Cafes, Bars):** 5pts vs old 20pts → **4X decrease**
- Each tier now shows estimated conversion rate & project value

**Why This Matters:**
- Focuses on clients who actually pay $8k+ (Tier 1-3)
- De-prioritizes low-budget local B2C (cafes, bars)
- Aligns with "niche down ruthlessly" research finding

### 3. **BUSINESS MATURITY SCORING (20 points) - REFRAMED**

**Research Finding:** "Target $100k+ revenue, 3-5 year established businesses"

**V3 Implementation:**

#### Revenue Proxy System (since actual revenue unavailable):
- **10-50 employees: 10pts** → Estimated $100k-$500k revenue
- **5-9 employees: 7pts** → Estimated $50k-$100k revenue
- **3-4 employees: 5pts** → Estimated $30k-$50k revenue
- **1-2 employees: 2pts** → <$30k revenue (risky)

#### Multi-Location Bonus:
- **2 locations: +5pts** → Proven model, expanding
- **3-5 locations: +8pts** → Successful chain, high budget
- **6+ locations: +4pts** → May have centralized marketing

#### Online Presence Paradox Scoring:
- **Active social + NO website: +10pts** → GOLDEN (understands marketing, needs site)
- **Active social + BAD website: +7pts** → EXCELLENT (ready for upgrade)
- **Google Reviews 20+: +4pts** → Established reputation

**Why This Matters:**
- Targets businesses that can actually afford professional design
- Recognizes that social media activity + poor website = high conversion
- Multiple locations = proven success = budget

---

### 4. **WEBSITE ISSUES (15 points max) - REDUCED WEIGHT**

**Research Finding:** "Industry and timing matter more than website issues"

**Change:** Reduced from 40pts → 15pts (62.5% decrease)

**Why?**
- A cafe with a terrible website is still a low-budget client
- A law firm with a mediocre website is a high-value opportunity
- Issues matter, but CONTEXT (industry + timing) matters more

**Critical Issues Still Tracked:**
- No SSL: 5pts
- Flash: 5pts
- Not mobile: 4pts
- Old copyright: 3pts
- Slow load: 3pts

**NEW: Modern Website Penalty**
- If ALL modern indicators present: **-10pts penalty**
- Signal: "Recently redesigned - SKIP"

---

### 5. **BUSINESS STATUS DETECTION - NEW FEATURE**

**Your Request:** "Make sure business is working, not permanently closed"

**V3 Implementation:**

#### Status Types:
1. **Permanently Closed**
   - Auto-reject: YES
   - Rejection reason: "Business permanently closed"

2. **Temporarily Closed**
   - Reduce score: 50%
   - Warning: "Verify before outreach"

3. **Open**
   - No penalty

4. **Unknown**
   - Assume open (benefit of doubt)

#### Detection Methods:
- **Google Business Profile API** (if available)
- **Google Maps scraping** (look for "Permanently closed" label)
- **Website keywords:**
  - "permanently closed"
  - "out of business"
  - "no longer operating"
  - "zavřeno natrvalo" (Czech)
  - "ukončili jsme činnost" (Czech)
- **Website offline:** Must fail 3 consecutive checks

**Why This Matters:**
- Saves time by filtering out dead businesses
- Prevents embarrassing outreach to closed businesses
- Focuses effort on active prospects

---

### 6. **ENHANCED AUTO-REJECT RULES**

**New Rejection Reasons:**

1. **Recently Redesigned**
   - Modern design + current copyright + domain <2 years old
   - They don't need help NOW

2. **DIY Free Platforms (Unlikely to Convert)**
   - wix.com/free
   - weebly.com
   - wordpress.com (NOT .org)
   - webnode.cz/free
   - carrd.co
   - **Research:** "Free DIY users typically won't pay for professional design"
   - **But wait...** DIY sites SHOULD be targets (they're ready to upgrade)
   - **Solution:** Don't auto-reject, but add +12 bonus points to redesign score!

3. **Corporate Franchise (No Local Autonomy)**
   - "corporate website only"
   - "see main franchise site"
   - No local marketing budget
