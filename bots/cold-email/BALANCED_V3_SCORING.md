# Balanced V3 Scoring System
## Context-Aware, Non-Strict, Give-Them-A-Chance Approach

---

## Philosophy Change

### ❌ OLD V3 (Too Strict):
- "No website = 0 points, reject"
- "Cafe = low priority, only 5 points"
- "Must be B2B professional services"
- Expensive APIs for every lead

### ✅ NEW Balanced V3:
- **Context matters**: No website + active business = investigate further
- **All businesses deserve a chance**: Unless government or recently redesigned
- **Cheap/free scraping**: No expensive APIs
- **Human judgment signals**: Give sales team context, not binary reject

## Balanced Scoring (100 points total)

### 1. Business Health Score (35 points max) - HIGHEST WEIGHT
**Philosophy**: Healthy businesses can afford websites, regardless of industry

#### Active Business Signals (20 points):
- ✅ Google Reviews 10+: **+10pts** (people know them)
- ✅ Recent reviews (last 3 months): **+5pts**
- ✅ Social media activity (posted last 30 days): **+5pts**
- ✅ Phone number listed & verified: **+3pts**
- ✅ Physical location (not home address): **+2pts**

**Context**: Vemsi cafe would score 20+ here (no website but 4.8 rating, active Facebook)

#### Revenue Signals (15 points):
- ✅ 5-20 employees: **+10pts** (estimated €50k-€200k revenue)
- ✅ 20-50 employees: **+8pts** (larger but still reachable)
- ✅ 3-5 employees: **+7pts** (small but viable)
- ✅ Multiple locations: **+5pts bonus**
- ⚠️ 1-2 employees: **+3pts** (sole proprietor, lower budget)
- ❌ 50+ employees: **+2pts** (too big, hard to reach decision maker)

**No API needed**: Scrape from Google Business listing, aresbusiness.cz

### 2. Website Redesign Opportunity (30 points max)

#### Scenario A: NO Website (15-30 points)
**Don't reject! Score based on context:**

- ✅ Active business (reviews/social) + NO website: **30pts** 🎯
  - **Why**: They need one! (Vemsi cafe = perfect example)
  - **Signal**: "Golden opportunity - active business missing online presence"

- ⚠️ Older business (5+ years) + NO website: **15pts**
  - **Why**: If they haven't gotten one yet, might not want one
  - **Signal**: "Low priority - established without web presence"

- ❌ New business (<6 months) + NO website: **10pts**
  - **Why**: Might be in progress or very limited budget
  - **Signal**: "Wait 6 months - too new"

#### Scenario B: HAS Website - Check Intent

**Step 1: Check if recently redesigned (AUTO-REJECT)**
- ✅ Modern design (viewport, HTTPS, responsive, fast)
- ✅ Copyright 2023-2025
- ✅ Clean code, professional framework (React, Next.js)
- ✅ Domain registered <2 years ago OR archive.org shows redesign

**Signal**: "Recently redesigned - SKIP"
**Score**: **-10pts penalty** (reject)

**Step 2: Check if professionally aspirational (LOW PRIORITY)**
Like bondcafe.cz - shows design INTENT even if poor execution:
- Has video backgrounds/animations
- Custom branding, professional copy
- Obvious investment in appearance
- Complex navigation attempts

**Signal**: "Already invested in professional design - unlikely to buy"
**Score**: **5-10pts** (low priority)

**Step 3: DIY/Outdated Website (PERFECT LEADS!)**
Like dimsumspot.cz - functional but amateur:
- ✅ DIY builder detected (Wix, Weebly, Wordpress.com): **+15pts**
- ✅ Copyright ≤2020: **+10pts**
- ✅ No HTTPS: **+8pts**
- ✅ Not mobile-friendly: **+8pts**
- ✅ Table layouts / Flash: **+10pts**
- ✅ Broken images/links: **+5pts**
- ✅ Very slow load (>5 seconds): **+5pts**

**Signal**: "DIY website - perfect redesign candidate"
**Max Score**: **30pts** (cap it)

### 3. Industry Fit (25 points max) - SIMPLIFIED

**Don't overcomplicate - just ask: "Can they afford €3k-€10k?"**

#### Tier 1: High Budget B2B (25 points)
- Legal, accounting, financial services
- Medical/dental practices, clinics
- Real estate agencies
- B2B consulting (IT, HR, marketing)
- **Why**: €8k-€15k budgets, 10-15% conversion

#### Tier 2: Medium Budget B2B (20 points)
- Architecture, engineering firms
- Insurance agencies
- B2B distributors/wholesalers
- Professional coaching/training
- **Why**: €5k-€10k budgets, 8-12% conversion

#### Tier 3: Customer-Facing Services (18 points)
- Restaurants (especially upscale like dimsumspot!)
- Hotels, boutique accommodations
- Gyms, fitness studios, wellness centers
- Beauty salons, spas, barbershops
- Event spaces, wedding venues
- **Why**: €3k-€8k budgets, need online presence for bookings

#### Tier 4: Retail & Trades (15 points)
- Retail stores (fashion, home goods)
- Specialized trades (plumbing, HVAC, electrical)
- Auto services (repair, detailing)
- **Why**: €2k-€5k budgets, varies widely

#### Tier 5: Small Local (10 points)
- Small cafes (like Vemsi!)
- Food trucks → brick & mortar
- Freelancers growing into agencies
- Local artisans/craftspeople
- **Why**: €1k-€3k budgets, but HIGH conversion if no website

#### Auto-Reject (0 points):
- ❌ Government, municipalities, schools
- ❌ Non-profits (unless clear commercial activity)
- ❌ Holding companies, investment firms
- ❌ Large corporations (50+ employees)

---

### 4. Timing Signals (10 points max)

#### Recent Changes (Good Timing):
- ✅ Business opened 3-5 years ago: **+8pts** (likely still on original site)
- ✅ New location opened recently: **+5pts** (expanding = budget)
- ✅ Rebranding detected (logo change, name): **+10pts** 🎯
- ✅ Hiring (job postings last 90 days): **+5pts** (growing)

#### Warning Signals (Bad Timing):
- ⚠️ "Temporarily closed" on Google: **0pts, flag for review**
- ⚠️ Multiple negative reviews mentioning closure: **0pts, flag**
- ⚠️ No social media updates >6 months: **-5pts**

**No expensive API**: Check Google Business, social media, job boards

## Implementation: NO Expensive APIs

### Free/Cheap Data Sources:

1. **Google Business Profile** (free scraping)
   - Reviews count, rating, recent reviews
   - Business status (open/closed)
   - Photos, phone, address

2. **aresbusiness.cz** (free API)
   - Employee count
   - Business age, registration date
   - Legal status

3. **Social Media** (free scraping)
   - Facebook: Last post date, follower count
   - Instagram: Last post date, follower count
   - No API needed - just scrape public pages

4. **Basic Website Analysis** (free)
   - HTTPS check (just check URL)
   - Mobile-friendly (viewport meta tag)
   - Copyright year (regex on footer)
   - DIY detection (URL contains wix/weebly, meta tags)
   - Load time (simple requests.get() timing)

5. **WHOIS** (free, rate-limited)
   - Domain age - only for top 20% of leads
   - Use free python-whois library
   - Cache for 30 days

6. **Archive.org** (free, rate-limited)
   - Last major redesign - only for borderline cases
   - Manual fallback for high-value leads

### What We DON'T Use:
- ❌ Paid screenshot APIs ($$$)
- ❌ Gemini/OpenAI for design analysis ($$$)
- ❌ PageSpeed Insights API (rate-limited, slow)
- ❌ Paid email validation APIs

1. ❌ Government entity
2. ❌ Recently redesigned (modern site, <2 years old)
3. ❌ Permanently closed (verified by multiple sources)
4. ❌ Large corporation (50+ employees)

### Otherwise, Score and Prioritize:
- **No website ≠ bad lead** (Vemsi cafe proof!)
- **Small cafe ≠ bad lead** (many can afford €2k-€5k)
- **DIY website = PERFECT lead** (dimsumspot example)
- **Professional intent = lower priority** (bondcafe example)

### The Key: Context Over Rules
- Active business + no website = opportunity
- DIY website + professional business = perfect fit
- Professional design attempt = less likely to buy
- Modern polished site = recently redesigned, skip
